02 / THE CODE

MY RULES

03 / STRATEGY & COMPOSURE

Principles inspired by Thomas Shelby's fictional strategic presence—not quotations attributed to him.

Composure. Observation. Timing.

01 — COMPOSURE
Do not let every emotion choose your response.

02 — OBSERVATION

03 — STRATEGY
Consider more than the first move—and think about its consequences.

04 — PRIVACY

05 — SELF-CONTROL
